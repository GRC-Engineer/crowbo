# Storage, retrieval and decision usefulness

Updated 29 September 2026. [Evaluation](EVALUATION.md) owns judgment qualification; [foundation](FOUNDATION.md#initial-workflows) owns the agreed workflow portfolio. This plan distinguishes the implemented access comparison from proposed remediation, issue/exception and workload studies. Assistant-authored expected answers remain unqualified. No new collection, transfer or live run is authorised by this document update.

## First test session

Retain the existing bounded access cases. Prepare distinct non-access remediation and issue/exception cases to test the portfolio, with an identifiable decision owner and obtainable evidence within its permitted processing scope. Selecting these cases is not a launch-order or commercial-entry decision. One access problem traversing all three workflows tests lifecycle continuity, not transfer across independent domains.

| Case family | Proposed initial contrast | Evidence needed to judge the answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remediation tracking | Treatment has progressed, but closure may depend on deployment or scoped verification. | Agreed treatment, affected scope, owner, dependencies, implementation/deployment/verification records, closure condition and relevant commitments/capacity. |
| Access reviews | Supported narrower arrangement versus required infrequent work, unavailable alternatives or missing deciding evidence. | Exact account and scope, required work, current permissions, dependencies, option feasibility and authority basis. |
| Issues and exceptions management | Candidate deviation with an existing issue, feasible treatment or permitted bounded exception; then changed justification or expiry. | Applicable requirement, actual practice, existing records, business need, safeguards and verification, authority, conditions, expiry and triggers. |

These are proposed development contrasts, not factual cases or qualified expected answers. Include supported action, justified deferral and an unresolved deciding fact. Do not reward information requests about facts already supplied. Third-party reassessment is a research comparator, not an initial application.

Before seeing output, record deciding facts, admissible alternatives, unacceptable omissions and changes that should alter the advice. Use the customer's risk method; distinguish current assessment from proposed improvement. Freeze criteria, source versions and assistance records. Missing facts stay visible. Counts of records do not prove coverage. Independent reviewers and unseen case families are needed for later qualification.

The earlier two-week prioritisation case remains development material. Its non-negotiable-commitment and capacity rules apply where supported, rather than defining every workflow's request. A deadline or urgent label alone still cannot establish non-deferral authority.

## Fair comparison

Run two separately reported comparisons:

| Comparison | Hold comparable | What it can establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Judgment on prepared facts | Question, evidence, constraints, permission scope and model configuration for method comparisons; include applicable rules/code and a capable model/skill. | Whether the method uses deciding facts, generates feasible alternatives and handles consequences, uncertainty and constraints better on those cases. |
| End-to-end customer work | The actual job and permitted source access/tools for the practitioner process, appropriate automation, a capable configured agent and Crowbo. | Retrieval, freshness, onboarding, active human effort, interruption, elapsed delay and operating cost for reaching a useful result. |

Include existing configured agents, such as Notion where available, with equivalent useful tools rather than substituting a weak prompt. Record unavailable comparators as untested. Record model/provider, prompt, criteria, source versions and any asymmetry in permissions or tool access. Preserve original outputs, retries, founder interventions and material corrections; manual onboarding is allowed but must count toward effort. A same-facts result does not establish lower end-to-end customer effort.

Assess material omissions, unsupported claims, unjustified approvals, infeasible proposals, defensible alternatives, reviewer correction minutes, elapsed time and metered usage. Do not score agreement with a single preferred answer as professional quality.

Repeat with workflow-relevant changes: required access task, deployment or verification result, exception validity or safeguard, owner authority, capacity/deadline, conflicting source and removed deciding fact. Use real changes where available; otherwise label counterfactual overlays and retain the source unchanged. Related variants are not independent samples. Check irrelevant wording/order changes separately.

Compare task-local evidence with relevant wider business/operational context, holding other factors fixed. Also add irrelevant context that should not change the conclusion. Credit finding and correctly using the deciding fact, not citation count, corpus size or agreement with the founder.

Compare Jev enabled/disabled after one reasoning baseline works. A stored classification is useful only if it improves the decision or reduces effort. More indexed records and valid JSON are not outcome evidence.

The current review command checks answer shape, citation identity and access/version binding. It explicitly does not prove complete intake or independent feasibility. Human review supplies those judgments in the first test.

## Access comparison

Freeze the case and candidate expectations before running these three methods:

1. A capable plain GLM prompt using the account-specific response contract.
2. The same model and contract with explicit access-decision guidance, without Jev.
3. The identical guided prompt with account-specific Jev assessments.

All three receive identical raw records, source revisions, account scope, decision context, model configuration and output budget. General ingestion assessments are excluded. No corrective feedback enters any arm. The runner verifies the schema hash, source hash, normalized complete user-payload hash, source bindings and configuration; the guided pair must have identical system-prompt hashes. The exact serialized user message also has a retained hash. Only the declared contextual assessments are excluded from the common-payload comparison.

This isolates the effect of supplying contextual Jev between methods two and three. Comparing method one with the guided methods also changes the guidance. One run per method across a few related development cases cannot establish a reliable advantage. Review useful supported action, failed alternatives, identity joins and targeted investigation separately. Do not count every information request as a success, or a valid output schema as a sound decision. Report failures and correction effort without silently replacing unsuccessful attempts.

## Engineering cases

The following cases describe existing engineering boundaries, not completed qualification of the new workflows. Before a new workflow is claimed, add observed checks for its domain conditions: merged versus deployed versus verified treatment, missing or expired exception authority, a rejected/revised choice, and shared case identity with contributor permissions intact. Test explicit reassessment when a condition changes; do not imply automatic monitoring.

| Case | Required observed behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated import | Same source/assessment; no unnecessary inference or embedding. |
| New question set, unchanged source | New matching assessment; old answers remain interpretable; embedding reused. |
| Failed Jev or embedding | Source persists; independent stage can finish; resume only missing work. |
| Changed source, stale search chunk | Return the current permitted revision or no result, never stale evidence as current. |
| Equal-time conflicting source | Current reasoning refuses unresolved input; authorised earlier review remains inspectable. |
| Missing required fact outside search top-k | Operator-selected review scope retains the obligation or declares the gap; no completeness claim. |
| Forbidden reader, processor or request route | No denied content enters inference or derived output. |
| Source or grant changes during reasoning | New result cannot be published as a valid current review. |
| Invalid criteria or invented citation | Reject before a provider call where possible, or before saving an accepted result. |
| Process restart | Preserve the API allowance and recover preparation from durable heads. |
| Historical inspection | Current contributor access is required; no model call; earlier evidence remains unchanged. |

Mocked HTTP and storage tests prove local behaviour only. Hosted persistence, native embedding and model execution need separate receipts. Production multiuser isolation, full retention cleanup, restore and concurrency are not established by the pilot.

## Measurements before expansion

Measure retrieval, valid result reuse, bounded fresh reasoning and deeper investigation separately. Report end-to-end median and tail latency with sample count and failures, including source-change-to-usable-reassessment time. Permission and changed-evidence checks remain part of reuse; query speed alone is not decision speed. Small samples do not establish stable tail performance.

Record ingestion, enrichment, indexing, reasoning, retries and maintenance costs, separating metered receipts from estimates. Record preparation, investigation, correction, review and interruption effort as active human time, distinct from elapsed delay. A local request reservation is not an invoice. Subsecond performance, cheap-model quality and a cost advantage remain hypotheses.

Qualify retrieval against a reviewed required-fact set. Keep keyword/semantic recall, missing constraints and incomplete populations separate. Add dataset size, concurrency and alternate databases only after a useful complete decision test shows what matters.

If a capable prompt performs as well, retain only demonstrated value in maintained evidence, reliable access, repeatability or reassessment. Numeric release thresholds remain unset until criteria and representative cases are calibrated.

Record customers returning with new cases, willingness to pay and meaningful decisions supported that previously went unexamined. Separate those observations from advisory output, implemented action and verified protection. More calls or notifications can increase burden; measure attention and unnecessary deferral as well as throughput.

## Workload study proposal

The portfolio is selected; demand, build order, commercial entry and later expansion require empirical work. A proposed initial study covers approximately 6–10 target organisations, using annual records/calendars for seasonality, recent case histories and short prospective diaries for work absent from tickets. This is neither an executed study nor a representative industry sample. Participation, evidence access and data processing need their own authorised scope.

Inventory customer contracts and assurance requests; access campaigns and between-campaign changes; inventory/scoping/ownership; control design, evidence, testing and retesting; issue triage, remediation and exceptions; risk assessments/reassessment; architecture and vulnerability decisions; policy maintenance, audits and management reporting; incident/exercise follow-up and awareness. Include third-party and SaaS/AI-use assessments as research comparators without adding vendor management to the build scope.

Use workflow → case → meaningful decision → actions/artifacts as the counting structure. A meeting, email and ticket about one decision are not three opportunities. Record required cadence, work performed, backlog, material changes and unexamined demand. For issues, distinguish open stock from new, changed, closed and reopened flows. For access, use scoped entitlements and investigation rate. For controls, do not multiply one shared implementation by its framework mappings.

Segment by headcount, GRC/control-owner capacity, systems, identities, vendors, business units, change rate, customer-assurance demand, regulatory exposure and maturity. Size alone does not determine volume. Split each activity into collection/reconciliation, explicit checks, interpretation, option comparison, recommendation, approval and follow-through; identify what is automated and where human correction remains.

Label observed records, sampled time, practitioner estimates and assumptions separately. Industry survey summaries and vendor-described capabilities are research context, not our measured workload or proof of advantage. Do not claim competitors lack context, citations, automation or lifecycle tracking. Choose later scope from useful decisions and customer effort rather than an unsupported activity ranking.
