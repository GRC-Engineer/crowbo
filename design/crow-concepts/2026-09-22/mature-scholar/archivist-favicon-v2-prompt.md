# Archivist favicon: small-size version

Created on 22 September 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. The first [head icon](archivist-favicon-v1.png) was simplified after checking it at actual browser-icon sizes.

The final [favicon PNG](archivist-favicon-v2.png) uses stronger lemon glasses and mint edges with fewer feather details. It is a small-size derivative of the selected Archivist; the main mascot artwork is unchanged. The generated source is 1254 × 1254 pixels, copied unchanged into the prototype. Browsers render it at their tab-icon size; prompt dimensions and pixel-grid counts are design targets, not measured export dimensions.

## Exact final prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Make a SMALL-SIZE OPTICAL VERSION of the attached Archivist crow specifically for a 16px browser favicon. Preserve this exact mature character: smooth adult crow crown, calm small eyes, right-facing three-quarter view, long slate-grey beak, lemon spectacles, mint edge on the viewer's left, violet-black plumage. Important: the reference has far too much tiny feather detail for a favicon. Rebuild it as clean authentic low-resolution pixel art on an actual 32 by 32 logical pixel grid, enlarged without smoothing for the deliverable. Every logical pixel is one large deliberate flat square. Reduce the entire head into ONLY 6–8 broad feather planes, no individual small feather texture, dithering, noise, gradients or shiny highlights. Use only 7 flat colours: opaque midnight #10101B background, crow ink #28243E, violet-slate #4D4568, muted light slate #77758D for the beak, mint #A4EDC3, lemon #EEF34B, and very dark #161522. Zoom into the HEAD with just a tiny neck base; remove most of the reference's long neck. The head and entire beak occupy nearly the whole square with one or two logical pixels of border. For recognisability at 16px, render the lemon spectacles as two crisp bright loops about six logical pixels wide each, with a single small mint eye mark inside each; keep the eyes restrained and adult, without eye whites or baby proportions. Make the mint contour on the left one or two logical pixels wide. The nose/beak and crown must fit inside the square. This is an icon-scale adaptation of the Archivist, not a new mascot and not a general illustrated portrait. Keep the background completely opaque solid midnight, no transparency or cutout. NO caption, letters, numerals, decorations, framing border, broad glow or shadow. Prefer a 512x512 square output showing the coarse 32-pixel grid clearly.
