What Crowbo does

One decision capability, applied to the work security teams already have.

Early access. The demo uses synthetic evidence and does not connect to your systems.

  1. 01

    Start from the question

    An access review, a remediation check, an exception request, or what deserves the team’s time next. Crowbo puts the business context around it.

  2. 02

    Evidence from the tools you already use

    Tickets, chat, code, identity and calendar records, each with its scope, freshness and provenance visible. Only permitted sources are used.

  3. 03

    Options, not just an answer

    Feasible responses compared side by side, with the deciding facts, what must hold, and what is still unresolved.

  4. 04

    Challenge it

    Correct a fact or add context and see what changes. Reassessment creates a new version and keeps the original basis.

  5. 05

    The owner decides

    A recommendation stays separate from the authorised decision and from execution. Crowbo does not change your systems.

  6. 06

    For people and agents

    The same decision capability through the workspace and through MCP tools, so assistants and pipelines can ask the same question.
