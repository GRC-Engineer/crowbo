From a difficult question
to a considered next step.

01

Start with the situation.

Bring the evidence, objectives and constraints into view. Be clear about what is still unknown.

02

Give the options a fair hearing.

Compare a purchase, a process change, more capacity or a better question. Follow what the evidence supports.

03

Leave a reason to return to.

Keep the assumptions and the decision together. Reconsider the call when something material changes.
