    02 / README.MD

    the answer needs a paper trail.

    less hand-waving. more working.

    Crowbo is being built for security decisions that need an explanation.

    Choosing a tool. Finding a bottleneck. Challenging an assumption. The point is to make the reasoning available to the person who has to own the call.

    [development] This is a design playground. The examples are authored simulations; they do not run a model or execute a decision.

    decision.record ← inspect the contract
    01 evidence +

    What supports the claim? Keep its source, scope, freshness and limits attached.

    02 alternatives +

    Compare feasible moves, including an existing process change and asking for missing information.

    03 uncertainty +

    Name the unresolved fact that could change the answer. Do not smooth it into a confidence score.

    04 authority +

    A recommendation proposes a next step. The accountable owner decides and authorises it.

    05 reassessment +

    When a material fact changes, revisit the decision and retain the earlier reasoning.
